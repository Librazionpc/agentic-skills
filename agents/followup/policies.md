# Follow-up Policies

- Follow-ups should be spaced according to SLA and channel rules.
- If no response after defined attempts, escalate per policy.

## SLA monitoring (spec)
- Gentle reminder when nearing breach.
- Escalation warning at 24 hours overdue (or policy-defined).
- Escalation trigger at 48 hours overdue (or policy-defined).

## Silent customer handling (spec)
- Send a check-in after 24 hours of silence.
- Schedule a final check-in per policy window.
- If still no response, mark as stale/pending closure and route for review if required.
