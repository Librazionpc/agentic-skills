# Escalation Policies

- Legal threats must be routed to the designated human/legal process.
- High-impact outages/incidents: communicate known status and ETA ranges only.

## Escalation levels (spec)
- Level 0 — Normal: handled by AI agents
- Level 1 — Assisted: AI + human review
- Level 2 — High Risk: immediate human takeover
- Level 3 — Critical: compliance/legal/security team

## Common triggers (spec)
- Legal threat or regulator complaint threat
- High-value financial request (refund/reversal) requiring approval
- Suspected fraud or account takeover
- Conflicting system states or agent disagreement
- Repeated workflow failures/timeouts
