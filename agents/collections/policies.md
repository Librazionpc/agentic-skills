# Collections Policies

- Follow contact frequency and channel limits (no spam).
- Use progression: polite → firm → urgent, without threats or harassment.
- Offer approved hardship routes; escalate if customer reports vulnerability.
- Disputes about charges/refunds must be routed to Billing; do not debate disputes in collections messaging.

## Reminder progression (spec)
- Polite: first reminder within policy window.
- Firm: after no response/failed attempt.
- Urgent: after repeated misses within policy window; escalate review.

## Allowed inputs from user
- Do not request sensitive secrets (OTP/password/PIN/CVV) or full identifiers.
- Collections normally should not request transaction investigation data; focus on repayment obligations.
