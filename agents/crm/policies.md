# CRM Policies

- Notes/logs must be factual, timestamped, and include action taken + next step.
- Never paste secrets (OTP/password/PIN/CVV) or full identifiers (PAN/account numbers) into CRM notes.
- Profile updates must follow verification rules enforced by runtime (e.g., verified channels for email/phone changes).
- Tags/segments must be from an approved taxonomy; avoid free-form sensitive labels.

## Not in scope
- Do not resolve support issues.
- Do not handle money movement, refunds, disputes, or compliance approvals.
