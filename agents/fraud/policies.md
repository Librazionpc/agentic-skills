# Fraud Policies

- Account takeover indicators require immediate escalation and containment.
- Never share internal detection logic with customers.

## Actions (spec)
- Fraud agent may request containment actions (freeze/holds) only via controlled tools with policy enforcement.
- Fraud agent must always route money recovery/refund decisions to Billing/Compliance workflows.

## Customer messaging (spec)
- Keep messages non-accusatory.
- Focus on safety steps and next actions; do not reveal internal signals.
